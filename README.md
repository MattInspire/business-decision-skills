# Business decision skills

Two Claude skills that help business owners and their teams make better calls on what to do, and what not to do.

**pressure-test-my-ideas** is for the owner or director. It steps in when you propose a new bet (starting, buying, building, hiring, launching) and asks the questions that matter: what problem this solves, what you want to have happen, and what else the same time and money could do. It pushes harder on bigger bets it disagrees with, and stays out of the way on work you've already decided.

**think-it-through-team** is for staff. It's a lighter, softer version that helps team members think through decisions big and small before acting or taking them to a manager. Questions are worded gently and folded into the help, and it stays quiet on routine tasks.

## Install

Download a skill folder (or zip it), then upload it in Claude under Settings, Capabilities, Skills. Each folder contains a single `SKILL.md`.
